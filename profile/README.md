## About

TreeSoft AI is the artificial intelligence division of **TreeSoft**, focused on researching and building AI systems that push past conventional approaches. We work close to the metal — model internals, training dynamics, and architecture-level experimentation — rather than just wrapping existing APIs.

<br>

## Focus Areas

- **Model Research** — interpretability, steering, and fine-tuning techniques
- **Applied AI Systems** — tools powered by custom models
- **Experimental Architectures** — exploring unconventional ideas others overlook
